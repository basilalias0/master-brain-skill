## What it shows
1. **No behaviour change on the worker side:**
   - All 9 runs tested only their own change.
   - All 9 skipped the slow full suite, and all 9 added tests.
   - The model already does this, at least when the README says the suite is slow. Law 7's worker clause costs nothing (tokens are level with prev) but gains nothing here.
2. **The one difference is the `scope: wide` flag:** 3/3 new runs set it for `shared/currencies.json`, so the Master would test before merging.
   - Every prev and control run mentioned the other readers of the file in free text.
   - The flag makes that machine-readable: `route-log` and the Master can act on it without reading prose.
3. **Found:** 4 of 6 skill runs wrote `test-guard: ok` although no run executed `test-guard.cjs`. That was new 1 of 3 and prev 3 of 3.
   - The field invites a false claim from workers. Only the Master runs test-guard (law 21).
   - **Proposal, not applied:** a worker's RESULT says `test-guard: Master's`, or drops the field.
4. **Cost:** new 68.6k against prev 69.1k agent tokens. Control is cheaper (63.4k) because it reads no skill files.
5. **The time saving is not measured here.** Law 7's time saving comes from the Master and Chief levels: the full suite runs once instead of once per chat. That needs a multi-worker run.
