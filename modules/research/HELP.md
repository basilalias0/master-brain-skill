# research help

Purpose: sourced answers with a search budget; separates fact, inference and unknown.
Use: `/master-brain research <question> [quick|standard|deep]` (3-5, 8-12, up to 20 searches).
Checks local logs first, then the web; tags every finding verified, reported or unverified.
Triggers: "research", "compare options", "is X true", "evaluate this library".
Example: `/master-brain research "queue for 10k jobs a minute" deep`
